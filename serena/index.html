Serena

/səˈriːnə/
晴空无云 · 心境澄明Clear skies, a quiet mind.

S
壹I

名字释义Meaning

serēnus, serēna
晴朗的，无云的；平静的，安详的clear, cloudless; calm, tranquil
—— 拉丁语形容词— Latin adjective

Serena 来自拉丁语形容词 serēnus，serēna 是它的阴性形式。这个词最早用来形容天空：晴朗、无云，后来引申到人：神情平静，内心安详从容[1]。

Serena comes from the Latin adjective serēnus; serēna is its feminine form. The word first described the sky — clear and cloudless — and was later used of people: a calm face, a settled and tranquil mind[1].

这个词再往上的来源，学界还没有定论。一种说法认为它和「干燥」有关，可以对照希腊语 ξηρός（干）：不下雨的天，就是晴天[1]。

Its deeper origin is uncertain. One proposal links it to a root meaning "dry" (compare Greek ξηρός) — a sky without rain is a clear sky[1].

从天空的晴朗，到人心的澄明。From a clear sky to a clear mind.

英语里的 serene（宁静的）、serenity（宁静），以及 serenade（小夜曲，经法语、意大利语来自拉丁语 serenare，本义「使晴朗、使平静」[2]），都和这个名字同源。

English serene, serenity and even serenade (via French and Italian, from Latin serenare, "to make clear, to calm"[2]) all share this root.

我赋予的现代解释What we hope it means for her

遇事不慌，心中有定力。看得清，想得明白，在任何环境里都能从容自处。

平静不是没有锋芒，而是不被一时的风雨左右。云散了，天自然是晴的。

Steady when things get hard, with a calm that comes from within. Seeing clearly, thinking clearly, and at ease wherever she is.

Calm is not the absence of strength; it is not being pushed around by passing storms. When the clouds clear, the sky is simply blue.

叁III

欧洲多语通用One word across Europe

Serena 最难得的一点：在好几种欧洲语言里，serena 本身就是一个日常形容词，即「平静的、晴朗的」的阴性形式。拼写一字不差，意思和名字完全一致[7]。

What makes Serena rare: in several European languages serena is itself an everyday adjective — the feminine of "calm, clear". Same spelling, same meaning as the name[7].

肆IV

以 Serena 为名的人Women named Serena

古代：帝国的公主Antiquity: an imperial princess

Serena 约 370 – 408c. 370 – 408
罗马帝国晚期的贵族女性，生于西班牙。她是皇帝狄奥多西一世的侄女，父亲去世后由狄奥多西收养。约 384 年嫁给名将斯提里科，两个女儿先后嫁给皇帝霍诺留，是当时帝国权力中心最有影响力的女性之一[9]。A noblewoman of the late Roman Empire, born in Hispania. Niece and adopted daughter of Emperor Theodosius I, she married the general Stilicho around 384; both her daughters became wives of Emperor Honorius. She was one of the most influential women at the centre of imperial power[9].

现代：从球场到太空Modern: from the court to orbit

Serena Williams 网球 · 1981–Tennis · b. 1981
中文常译「塞雷娜·威廉姆斯」，人称「小威」。她拿下 23 座大满贯女单冠军，是公开赛年代的最高纪录；还有 4 枚奥运金牌，世界第一累计 319 周[10]。她在顶尖竞技中的冷静和强大，是对这个名字最好的诠释。23 Grand Slam singles titles — the most of the Open Era — plus four Olympic gold medals and 319 weeks at world No. 1[10]. Composure and power at the very top of sport.

美国医生、工程师、NASA 宇航员。2018 年 6 月至 12 月搭乘联盟 MS-09 飞船，在国际空间站执行第 56、57 期任务，在太空工作了将近 197 天[11]。名字本义是「晴朗的天空」，她真的飞到了天空之上。American physician, engineer and NASA astronaut. From June to December 2018 she flew Soyuz MS-09 to the International Space Station for Expeditions 56/57, spending nearly 197 days in space[11]. A name that means "clear sky" — and she went beyond it.

英语读作 /səˈriːnə/，三个音节，重音在中间。拼写和读音一一对应，第一次见到的人基本不会读错、写错。

In English: /səˈriːnə/, three syllables, stress on the middle one. It is spelled exactly as it sounds, so people rarely misread or misspell it.

更巧的是，意大利语 /seˈre.na/、西班牙语 /seˈɾena/、葡萄牙语里，重音同样落在第二个音节，只是元音略有不同。走到哪里，这个名字的节奏都一样。

In Italian /seˈre.na/, Spanish /seˈɾena/ and Portuguese the stress also falls on the second syllable; only the vowels shift slightly. Wherever she goes, the name keeps its rhythm.

和姓连读With the family name

Serena Li：名字以开口元音 -a 收尾，接上 Li 的辅音 l，过渡自然，不粘连。三个音节加一个音节，长短得当。

Serena Li: the open -a ending flows cleanly into the l of Li; three syllables then one — a balanced rhythm.

在亚洲In Asia

中文音译为「塞雷娜」，日语里写作 セリーナ / セレナ。约从 1980 年代起，Serena 在日本也成了女孩名，用汉字写作「星礼奈」「世伶奈」等[8]。

Rendered 塞雷娜 in Chinese and セリーナ / セレナ in Japanese. Since around the 1980s Serena has also been a Japanese girls' name, written with kanji such as 星礼奈 or 世伶奈[8].

陆VI

面向世界的名字A name for the world

我们为她选一个英文名，希望的不只是好听，而是走到世界任何地方，都不需要被翻译、被解释：

We wanted more than a pretty English name. We wanted one that needs no translation and no explanation, wherever in the world she goes:

◆ 在欧洲，在罗马、马德里、里斯本、巴塞罗那，serena 就是「平静的」；在伦敦、纽约，人们听到它会想起 serene；在巴黎，它是 sereine 的姐妹词。

◆ 在世界语里，这门专为跨国交流设计的语言中，serena 的意思也正是「平静的」。

◆ 在美洲，智利有一座城市就叫拉塞雷纳（La Serena），1540 年代建城，得名于西班牙的同名地区[12]。

◆ 在亚洲，日本有以 Serena 为名的女孩，菲律宾他加禄语也借用了这个名字[7]。

◆ In Europe — in Rome, Madrid, Lisbon and Barcelona, serena simply means "calm"; in London and New York it echoes serene; in Paris it is a sister of sereine.

◆ In Esperanto, the language built for people of every nation, serena means exactly "calm".

◆ In the Americas, the Chilean city of La Serena, founded in the 1540s, is named after a region of Spain[12].

◆ In Asia, Serena is a girls' name in Japan and has been borrowed into Tagalog in the Philippines[7].

一个名字，从罗马人的晴空出发，穿过两千年、跨过几大洲，意思始终不变。我们希望她也一样：无论身处哪种文化、哪种语言，都能从容自在，心境澄明。

A word that began as a Roman sky has crossed two thousand years and several continents without changing its meaning. We hope the same for her: in any culture, in any language, to be at ease and clear of mind.

柒VII

与「含章」相合Serena & Hanzhang

她的中文名「含章」出自《周易》：才华蕴藏于内，待时而发。英文名 Serena 出自拉丁语：心境澄明，从容自若。

一个来自东方最古老的经典，一个来自西方最古老的语言之一；一个讲内在的积累，一个讲外在的从容。正因为心里有积累，才能遇事不慌；正因为不慌，才能等到对的时机。

Her Chinese name, 含章 Hanzhang, comes from the Book of Changes: brilliance held within, revealed in its own time. Serena comes from Latin: a clear, unhurried calm.

One from the oldest classic of the East, one from a foundational language of the West. One speaks of what is gathered within, the other of the calm it shows without. Because there is depth inside, she need not hurry; because she does not hurry, she can wait for the right moment.
